About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Weber State University where I am studying pedagogy and developing my reserach program which focuses on invasive species ecology and restoration. I graduated from Montana State University in 2026 with my PhD in Ecology and Environmental Sciences and from Gustavus Adolphus College in 2021 with my BA in Biology and a focus in Biochemistry.
I strongly believe in the power of a liberal arts education and aspire to integrate research opportunities into my classrooms to enhance the learning of the next generation of scientists.
