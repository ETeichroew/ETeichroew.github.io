Teaching Philosophy

My goal is to help students grow into strong, confident versions of themselves while preparing them for success in their futures. My teaching philosophy is centered on the principles of hands-on learning, creating a safe learning environment, and developing effective communication skills. I do my best to ensure students have opportunities to learn imporant academic and real world skills while being able to laugh along the way.

Weber State University - BTNY 1403 (Sp 27)

Course focused on interactions between humans and our environment, covering topics including ecology, evolution, resource management, pollution, and agriculture. Included a lab section supplementing lecture content.

Biodiversity Survey and Montioring Methods

Montana State University - ENSC 410 (F 25)

Research-based course incorporating a week of hands-on field work learning survey design and sampling strategies of different taxa. Included an introduction to data analysis and visualization in R and an emphasis on scientific writing skills culminating in an independent research paper.
